# WordPress Staff Training Manual: News & Board Management

This guide explains how non-technical organization staff can log in, publish news, upload photos, and update or reorder the Board of Directors **without needing a developer or writing any code**.

---

## 1. How Staff Log In

1. Open your browser and go to:
   ```
   https://organization.lk/wp-admin
   ```
2. Enter your authorized staff username/email and password.
3. Click **Log In**. You will land on the **WordPress Administration Dashboard**.

---

## 2. How to Add & Publish News Articles

The organization uses the built-in WordPress **Posts** system for press releases, events, and announcements.

### Step 1: Open the Editor
* In the left sidebar, click **Posts** -> **Add New Post**.

### Step 2: Enter Headline & Content
* **Title:** Type the headline (e.g., `Board of Directors Approves 2026 Expansion Roadmap`).
* **Content:** Use the WordPress block editor:
  * Type text normally for paragraphs.
  * Click **+** to insert **Headings** (H2, H3), **Quotes**, **Lists**, or **PDF Download links**.

### Step 3: Choose Category
* In the right sidebar under **Post**:
  * Check the appropriate category: **Press Release**, **Event**, **Announcement**, or **Governance**.

### Step 4: Upload Cover Image
* In the right sidebar, scroll down to **Featured image**.
* Click **Set featured image**.
* Drag and drop a photo from your computer or phone, or select an existing one from the Media Library.
* Click **Set featured image**.

### Step 5: Save or Publish
* **To publish immediately:** Click the blue **Publish** button in the top right.
* **To save for review:** Click **Save draft**. You can return and publish it later.
* **Confirmation:** Visit `organization.lk/news` to confirm your article is live!

## 3. How to Manage the Board of Directors

The organization has a dedicated **Board of Directors** menu located right on the left sidebar.

### Adding a New Director (e.g. Appointment of a New Chairman)
1. In the left sidebar, click **Board of Directors** -> **Add New Director**.
2. **Title:** Type the director's full name with honors (e.g., `Dr. Senaka Wickramasinghe`).
3. **Biography:** Type their executive profile and credentials in the main text box.
4. **Position / Title:** In the *Board Member Official Credentials* box below, enter their designation (e.g., `Chairman of the Board`).
5. **Display Hierarchy Order:**
   * Enter **`1`** for Chairman
   * Enter **`2`** for Vice Chairperson
   * Enter **`3`**, **`4`**, **`5`** for other directors.
   * *This ensures the Chairman always displays first on the public website.*
6. **Membership Status:** Leave as **Active Board Member**.
7. **Portrait Photo:** In the right sidebar under **Featured image**, click **Set featured image** and upload a square/portrait photo of the director.
8. Click **Publish**. The new board member is now immediately live on `organization.lk/directors`!

### Reordering the Board of Directors
To change who appears first or adjust hierarchy:
1. Go to **Board of Directors** -> **All Directors**.
2. Click on the director you wish to adjust.
3. In the *Display Hierarchy Order* box, update the number (e.g., change `4` to `2`).
4. Click **Update**.
5. The public website updates the order automatically.

### When a Director Steps Down (Preserving Historical Memory)
**Do NOT permanently delete the record.** Instead:
1. Open the director's profile under **Board of Directors**.
2. Change **Membership Status** from *Active Board Member* to **Past / Emeritus Member (Historical)**.
3. Enter their service dates under **Tenure** (e.g., `2018 – 2024`).
4. Click **Update**.
5. The former director will automatically move to the *Emeritus & Past Board Members* section on the public website, preserving organizational history and respect for past leadership!

## 4. Updating Organization Phone, Email, & Address

To change the contact numbers or physical address shown on the website:
1. In the left sidebar, go to **Appearance** -> **Customize**.
2. Click **Organization Information & Contacts**.
3. Update the telephone, public hotline, email, or headquarters address.
4. Click **Publish** at the top. The top bar, contact page, and footer update instantly.
